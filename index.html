Governance & Security

Fail-Closed by Design. A Gate in Front of Everything.

Built for regulated industries — banking, capital markets, insurance — where every access decision must be defensible.

Identity

Each caller maps to a target account via your SSO — no shared logins, no anonymous access, no open-ended credentials.

Entitlements

Roles decide exactly which systems and operations a caller may reach. Agent permissions mirror your existing RBAC.

Vaulted

Secrets stay in the vault. Sessions open just-in-time and fail closed if unmapped — no mapping, no call.

Audited

Every call logged with an immutable trail — inputs, outputs, policy decisions, and lineage. Evidence, not assurances.
